 Potential Delays due to Amtrak Infrastructure Project

Due to an Amtrak infrastructure project near William H. Gray III 30th St Station: Paoli/Thorndale, Cynwyd, Trenton, Chestnut Hill West, Fox Chase and West Trenton trains may experience delays of up to 10 minutes through early November. Please plan for added travel time. SEPTA apologizes for the inconvenience.
