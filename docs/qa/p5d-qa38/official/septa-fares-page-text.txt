You must use the same card for all segments of your trip to receive free transfers. Please note that riders paying with cash or Quick Trip are not eligible for free transfers. Instead, they can simply walk from one platform to the other. This benefit is available to all riders, regardless of how they paid their fare. More Information about Free Transfers: Free transfers are available only between SEPTA Bus & Metro services. They are not available for trips involving Regional Rail. Similarly, any transfer to Regional Rail or from Regional Rail will require an additional fare. The 2-hour free transfer window is valid for round-trips. This applies to return trips on the same route or a parallel route.
